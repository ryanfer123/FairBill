FairBill reviews hospital bill and insurance documents you choose to provide.

What we use

We use your email to sign you in. We process uploaded photos and PDFs, pasted text, your corrections, and case findings to review charges and prepare drafts and evidence packets.

Where it goes

Your account and cases are stored with Supabase in Mumbai. The review service runs on Render in Singapore and processes the documents you submit. When you use your phone to send a letter, call, or share a packet, the selected app or service handles that action.

How long we keep it

Cases, extracted text and uploaded originals are scheduled for deletion 30 days after the case is created. Your email account remains available until you delete it. Files or messages you choose to export are managed by the app you share them with.

Delete your data or contact us

Use the account settings in FairBill or delete your account online. This removes your account, cases and stored originals. For privacy questions, contact {contact}.

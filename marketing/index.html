The patient workflow

From bill
to next step.

You confirm the records. FairBill does the arithmetic. You decide what to share.

Scan and confirm

Take a photo, upload a file, or paste itemised text. Check extracted charges against the original.

Find the questions

Check repeated charges, quantities, and insurer deductions. Open the source lines behind a finding.

Prepare your response

Review a billing letter or call script, or save a redacted evidence PDF. FairBill doesn’t contact anyone on your behalf.

Privacy and limits

Your bill is yours.

Does this website take uploads?

No. This site explains FairBill with a fictional example. It never asks for a bill or personal information.

Does a flagged charge mean I was overcharged?

No. A finding is a question for the billing team. The conditional total shows what could change if the questions are accepted, not confirmed savings.

How do I get the Android app?

Use the Android build instructions in the project’s GitHub repository. This page does not claim a Play Store release.
